# Desktop Review Specification (delta)

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A Pair The Model Doubted Can Still Be Combined

When an identity decision judged uncertain or different lists merge among its actions, the line above the question MUST carry the model's doubt followed by "If you know they are the same, you can still combine them, and split them again later.", the model's reason MUST stay under "Why the AI model thinks so", and Same thing MUST preview the combine.

#### Scenario: A pair the model doubted can still be combined

- GIVEN an identity decision over a pair judged uncertain or different whose actions include merge
- WHEN it is opened
- THEN the doubt and the override line are shown and the options are Same thing, Different things and Decide later

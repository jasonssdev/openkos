## Decisions (owner, and orchestrator under the owner's autonomy grant)

- `status: stable` replaces `active`, and readers accept a legacy `active`.
- Deprecation stays computed from `supersedes` edges: nothing writes `status: deprecated` (follow-up #1075).
- `provenance` stays the internal source of truth; `sources` is a one-way projection that is never read back as trust input.
- Migrated documents get `generated.by: openkos/legacy`, with `at` set to the old `timestamp` text exactly as written.
- `repair` migrates in place in one commit, including the merge ledger; `okf_version` flips last in the same commit.
- Hand-written `# Citations` content is left in place and reported, never converted.
- Per-claim `[^id]` footnotes, `verified`, `stale_after` and Attested Computation are deferred.

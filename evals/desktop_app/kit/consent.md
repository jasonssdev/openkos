# Consent: OpenKOS test session

Read this with the participant. Give them time to read it. They keep a copy.

## What this is

You are helping to test an app called OpenKOS. We are testing the app, not you. If something is hard to do, that is exactly what we need to learn.

## What will happen

- A session of about one to one and a half hours. You will be asked to install the app on a computer, add two files to it, ask it a question and make one decision it asks you for.
- I will not be able to help you or answer questions while you work. You may use the web as you would at home.
- Your screen and your voice will be recorded for the whole session.
- Afterwards I will ask you two short questions about what you did.

## What happens to the recording and your answers

- The recording stays private with me. It is never published.
- What is published is a written summary: times, whether each task worked, quotes of things you said, and notes. It does not include your name or anything that identifies you. Quotes are published word for word, so please do not say anything you would not want quoted.
- The two files you will use are made up and hold no personal information. You will not be asked to enter any of your own.

## Your choices

- You can stop at any point, for any reason, without explaining. Just say so.
- You can ask me to delete the recording at any time, including after the session, and I will.
- You can decline to be recorded or to have the anonymized results published. If you do, the session cannot go ahead.

## Agreement

I have read this page. I agree to the screen and audio recording, and to the anonymized results being published.

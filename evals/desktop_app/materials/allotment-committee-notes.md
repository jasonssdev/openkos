# Riverside Allotments — committee meeting notes

Present: Marguerite Osei (chair), Tom Haldane, Priya Raman, Joe Whitlock.

## Water

The old pump at the north tank failed twice in August. The committee compared three replacements and chose the **AquaLift 330**, because it runs on the existing solar panel and the supplier will fit it before the first frost. Tom will collect it from the depot and Joe will fit it with the supplier's engineer.

## Plot waiting list

Eleven households are waiting for a plot. Priya proposed splitting the two overgrown plots at the east fence into four half plots, so that four households can start in spring instead of two. Agreed, subject to clearing the brambles first.

## Open day

The open day moves to the second Saturday in June. Marguerite Osei will ask the primary school whether a class can plant the herb bed.

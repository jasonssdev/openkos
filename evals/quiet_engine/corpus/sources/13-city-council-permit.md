# Harbour committee permit — pole 14

Notes from the Port Alder town harbour committee meeting of 2026-04-22, where the Harbor Weather Network asked for permission to mount a station on pole 14 on the quay.

## Attendees

- Councillor Sam Patel, chair of the harbour committee.
- Councillor Ruth Abara.
- The town engineer.
- Ana Ruiz, for the Port Alder Makers.

## The request

Ana presented the project: a small solar-powered weather station on the lamp post nearest the harbour office, publishing wind and temperature for the sailing school, the harbour office and the public. She brought a finished station so the committee could see the size.

## Concerns raised

- **Load on the pole.** The town engineer checked the pole's rating. A station with its panel weighs under eight kilograms and adds very little wind load. Acceptable.
- **Lighting.** The station must not shade the lamp or block the camera on the next pole.
- **Responsibility.** Councillor Patel asked who maintains the station and who removes it if the project ends. The Port Alder Makers will maintain it and remove it at their cost.
- **Data.** Councillor Abara asked whether the station records people. It does not: there is no camera and no microphone, only weather sensors.

## Decision

The committee approved the request. The council approved a mounting height of 6 metres on pole 14, above the lamp head, with a clamp of the town's approved type. The permit is valid for three years and can be renewed.

## Conditions

1. Installation by a contractor on the town's list, or under the town engineer's supervision.
2. The station is labelled with the project's contact details.
3. The Port Alder Makers carry public liability insurance for the station.
4. The station is removed within thirty days if the town needs the pole.

## Follow-up

Ana will arrange the installation with the town engineer. Councillor Patel asked to be sent the dashboard link, which the harbour committee may link from the town website.

## Other items on the committee's agenda

The station was item four of seven. The committee also discussed the dredging schedule for the inner harbour, the renewal of the fishing cooperative's berth lease, and a complaint about parking on the quay during the summer festival. Councillor Patel noted that the weather station's wind readings could help the harbour office decide when to close the outer pontoon in a gale, which the committee had argued about the previous winter.

# Volunteer onboarding

How a new volunteer joins the Harbor Weather Network. The Port Alder Makers run build nights every Tuesday at the Quay Street workshop; anyone can come.

## First evening

1. Meet Ana Ruiz or whoever is running the build night.
2. Read the project overview and the hardware guide.
3. Sit with an experienced volunteer while they assemble or repair a station.
4. Sign the workshop safety sheet; soldering irons and the pillar drill have their own rules.

## Second evening

1. Assemble a station's sensor board from the parts list, supervised.
2. Run the bench test.
3. Help with a sensor calibration, using the procedure on the calibration page.

## Accounts

Volunteers who will work on stations get a login to the shared notes. Only Tomas Weber and Ana Ruiz have accounts on the club server; volunteers do not need one. Nobody gets the broker passwords; stations are provisioned by Sam Patel.

## Going up a pole

Nobody works at height alone. Installations on the quay follow the town's conditions: the contractor or the town engineer is present. On the sailing school and lifeboat sites, two volunteers and a ladder that is tied off.

## Students

Students from Port Alder High School come with Lena Okafor, their teacher. They do not use the soldering irons or work at height. They build sensor boards, run calibrations, and read the data for their coursework.

## Code of conduct

The Port Alder Makers' code of conduct applies at build nights and in the shared notes: be kind, share tools, put things back.

## A volunteer's first month

Most volunteers settle into one of three roles within a month. Builders assemble and repair stations at the workshop. Visitors go out to the sites to clean radiation shields, check anemometer cups and look at the panels. Watchers keep an eye on the dashboards and the data quality checklist and raise a flag when something looks wrong. A volunteer can be all three; the point of the roles is that every station has somebody who visits it and somebody who watches it.

## Leaving

Volunteers who stop coming keep their login to the shared notes for six months. After that the account is closed. Nothing they wrote is deleted.

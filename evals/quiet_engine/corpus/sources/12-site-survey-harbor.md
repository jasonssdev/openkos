# Site survey — harbour front

Ana Ruiz and Priya Nair walked the harbour front on 2026-04-08 to choose mounting points for the waterfront stations.

## Breakwater

The end of the breakwater is the most exposed point in town and the site the sailing school cares about most. There is a disused navigation light pole near the end that the harbour office no longer uses. It is solid steel, but salt has eaten the base plate; it needs a new clamp. Wi-Fi does not reach; the LoRa link does.

## Sailing school

The boathouse roof has a clear view to the west and south-west, which is where squalls come from. A short mast on the gable end would put the anemometer well above the roof. Mains power is available, but we will use solar anyway so that every station is identical.

## Harbour office

The office has a flagpole on the quay side. The harbour master is happy for us to use it, but any change to street furniture on the quay belongs to the town, not the harbour office. Councillor Sam Patel, who chairs the town's harbour committee, came by during the survey and said the committee would need to approve a permanent mount on pole 14, the lamp post nearest the office.

## Lifeboat station

The lifeboat crew offered the top of their slipway shelter. Good exposure to the north, sheltered from the south by the cliff, so its wind readings will be lower in a southerly. We will note that on the dashboard rather than move it.

## Cannery roof

The old cannery is being converted to flats. The developer is willing to host a station during the works. The roof is sheltered by a parapet wall on one side; not suitable for a rain gauge.
